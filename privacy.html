Privacy Policy

FRamily Holiday Fun · Brand: FRamily (operated by the Dunson family) · Effective November 9, 2026

This page describes how FRamily ("we", "us") collects and uses information on the FRamily Holiday Fun RSVP website and related SMS program.

What we collect

  • Family or guest name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Which events you RSVP to, how many people are coming, and whether payment was sent
  • Your language preference (English or Spanish)

How we use it

  • To confirm your RSVP and reserve lanes, bays, tickets or seats
  • To send you SMS confirmations and event reminders for the events you chose, if you opted in
  • To coordinate payment for events with a per-person cost

SMS program

If you check the SMS consent box on the RSVP page, we will send you text messages about the events you RSVP to. Message frequency varies, typically 1 to 3 messages per event. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time. Reply HELP for help.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Phone numbers collected for SMS consent are used only to send the messages described above.

Who sees your information

Only the event hosts (FRamily and family). Information is stored with our service providers (hosting and messaging) solely to operate the RSVP site and send messages. It is never sold or used for advertising.

How long we keep it

RSVP and contact data is deleted after the event series ends, no later than February 28, 2027.

Your choices

You can change or remove your RSVP at any time from the same link. To delete your information or ask a question, text or call Jared Dunson at 954-376-0188.
